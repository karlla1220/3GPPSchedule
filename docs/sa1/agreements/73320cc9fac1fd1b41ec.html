Drafting p-CRs:

  • All changes must be shown using revision marks against existing text in the draft TS/TR, otherwise p-CRs may be Noted

  • For more info: https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/tsg_sa/WG1_Serv/Delegate_Guidelines_v2_.doc and https://www.3gpp.org/delegates-corner/faqs

When writing CRs, please follow the guidance provided in S1-263007 (Guidelines to write CRs)

Only editable figures in normative specifications will be accepted. Figures can be made with MS Visio, WPS, to make sure future editability is possible when needed during TS maintenance in next releases. Not allowed: non-editable figures (e.g. AI generated figures). For file size concerns, photos will not be allowed in any form (e.g. even embedded in figures).

Requirements shall be implementable and quantifiable as much as possible, e.g. no requirement as “This shall be supported efficiently”.

Wording shall be clear, unambiguous, using active form rather than passive as much as possible.

Delegates are expected to upload their revisions before the meeting is closed.
