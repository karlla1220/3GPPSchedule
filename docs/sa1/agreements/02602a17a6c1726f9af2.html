S1-263470

China Telecom

New use case on accessibility parameters for IMS multimedia emergency call

Approved

Revision of S1-263392.

The only change is to have first and second requirement with the following text:

[PR 5.1.6-1] The 3GPP system shall enable a user to set accessibility parameters in the UE or in the network for an IMS emergency call or an IMS MES including: modality (e.g. signed, written or spoken language) and language preferences for MO and MT, and address of any preferred assisting service (e.g. video assisting service between sign language and speech).

[PR 5.1.6-2] The 3GPP system shall support establishing a multiparty IMS emergency call or an multiparty IMS MES (e.g. with PSAP and assisting services) considering the accessibility parameters, by the UE or the originating network.
