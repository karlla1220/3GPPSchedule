Note 1: Including design of PRACH and transmission, and RACH procedure

[127-R20-6GR-PRACH] Email discussion on Rel-20 6GR-PRACH and RACH procedure – Daewon (IDC)

  • To be used for sharing updates on online/offline schedule, details on what is to be discussed in online/offline sessions, tdoc number of the moderator summary for online session, etc

Conclusion

  • There is no consensus on need for support of more than 64 preambles per RO for increasing preamble capacity.

Agreement

  • Use NR ZC PRACH sequence design, including the applicable unrestricted/restricted sets and sequence lengths, as the baseline 6GR sequence.

  • Identify the deployment conditions in which sequences do not meet requirements and study how to address, e.g., enhanced single-sequence constructions, and dual-/multi-sequence constructions, with ZC based and non-ZC based sequences.

Agreement

For 2-step RACH study,

  • Physical-layer design of 2-step RACH uses NR 2-step RACH (MsgA PRACH, MsgA PUSCH and MsgB) as the starting point.

    • Correspondence between MsgA PRACH (preamble/RO) and MsgA PUSCH/DMRS, including the mapping ratio.

For RACH-less/contention-based PUSCH (CB-PUSCH) access study, further study the following:

  • Target UEs and scenarios, e.g., stationary/low-mobility UEs (e.g., FWA, mIoT such as meters and sensors), and high RSRP UEs, and NTN UEs with GNSS-based timing/frequency pre-compensation.

For dedicated RACH (1-step) using a UE-dedicated PRACH preamble study, further study the following:

  • Applicable RRC states and use cases (e.g., paging-triggered RA, grant-free/shared data transmission, scheduling request for UEs without valid TA).

  • Assumptions for the number of users in the network and the number of sequences needed for UE dedicated PRACH preamble and justification of feasibility.

  • Detection performance to detect correct UE dedicated PRACH preamble

On the justification of the additional procedure(s) study,

  • UE RRC states and benefits (e.g., latency, signaling overhead, UE/network energy) relative to 4-step RACH, considering the resource/configuration overhead and UE/BS complexity of supporting 4-step RA and other procedures in a cell.
