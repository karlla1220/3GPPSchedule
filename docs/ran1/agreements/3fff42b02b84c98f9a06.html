Agreement:

In 6GR, for single TRP case, the UE is not expected to be scheduled to transmit a PUSCH that is scheduled with a DCI that overlaps in time with another PUSCH that is scheduled with another DCI in the same carrier.

In 6GR, for single TRP case, a PUSCH that is scheduled with a DCI (DG-PUSCH) overrides an time-overlapping CG-PUSCH on the same carrier, provided that a timeline requirement is satisfied.

R1-2610704 FL summary#3 on handling of potential multiple uplink transmissions Moderator (MediaTek)

Agreement:

For the study of defining a deterministic timeline for processing collision-handling, the timeline for processing collision-handling is independent of the UL channels/signals/UCI scheduled/configured within the time-unit

  • Note: “separate CSI measurement/computation triggering/configuration and CSI reporting triggering/configuration” was agreed in RAN1

  • The collision-handling timeline doesn’t consider the CSI measurement and computation process

  • The collision-handling timeline needs to consider the timeline for CSI report triggering and transmission

  • Note: details for the timeline for collision-handling at least depend on the details for “separate CSI measurement/computation triggering/configuration and CSI reporting triggering/configuration” in the CSI agenda

Agreement:

For the study of one-shot collision-handling (multiplexing/prioritization) for PUCCHs/UCIs based on a time-unit, consider following options.

  • Option 1: collision-handling is performed for all PUCCHs/UCIs without repetition within the time-unit, irrespective of symbol-level overlap among the associated transmissions.

  • Option 2: collision-handling is performed for all PUCCHs/UCIs without repetition within the time-unit in case that there is overlapping between at least two PUCCHs. Otherwise, all the PUCCHs transmitted in a TDM manner.

  • Option 3: collision-handling is performed for all PUCCHs/UCIs without repetition within the time-unit in case that there is overlapping between at least two PUCCHs or there are more than 3 PUCCHs in the time-unit. Otherwise, all the PUCCHs/UCIs transmitted in a TDM manner.

  • Option 4: collision-handling is performed for all semi-static PUCCHs/UCIs without repetition within the time-unit, and dynamic PUCCH/UCI if it overlaps with any of the resources for semi-static PUCCH(s)/UCI(s).

  • Option 6: One common list of PUCCH resources for all the UCIs in a time-unit.

    • A PUCCH resource is selected based on all the UCIs (after multiplexing/prioritization) in the time-unit

    • There is no dedicated PUCCH resource allocation or configuration per UCI type

R1-2610705 FL summary#4 on handling of potential multiple uplink transmissions Moderator (MediaTek)

For network-signalling methods to simplify or mitigate ambiguities in collision-handling procedures for UL channels, signals, and/or UCI, study (till RAN1#128) the scenarios, purposes, and necessity where network signalling may be beneficial, including, for example:

  • To control the channel(s) for UCI multiplexing in the case of multiple channels.

  • To control the UCI to be multiplexed in the PUSCH/PUCCH

  • To exclude an UL channel from collision handling procedure within a time-unit for TDMed UL transmissions, (if TDMed channels within a time-unit is supported)

  • Scenarios with ambiguity in determining the target UL transmission for UCI multiplexing.

  • Consider single-carrier and multi-carrier operation
