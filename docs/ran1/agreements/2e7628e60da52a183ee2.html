Working assumption:

The following options based on CP-OFDM enhancement, if the necessity and benefits are identified, are considered for further study on NR ZC sequence optimization for 6G ISAC.

  • Root index optimization of NR ZC sequence

    • add q = ± 1 (equivalent to q = 1 or NZC-1)

    • non-uniform root index, q=⌊̅q+12⌋+v(−1)⌊2̅q⌋, for v=0,1 and ̅q=̅qPSLR=(NZC−3)(umod2)+(−1)u∙2⌊u2⌋+1, for u=0,1,…,29

    • root index qf in frequency domain corresponding to the root index qt∈{+1,+2,…,+K/2,Nzc−1,Nzc−2,…,Nzc−K/2} (K=numberofZCsequences) in time domain, subject to qf=NZC−r, where (r*qt)modNzc=1

    • change (u+1)/31 to u/30

    • root index and group number selection based on full search

    • Joint-optimized (PAPR, doppler induced range sidelobes, and cross correlation) root selection

    • q={qv=0}∪{qv=1}, where

      • qv=0=round(Nzc*hk), where k=u+1, u={1…29},,and h=Nzc−1(modk), which means (h*Nzc)modk=1.

      • qv=1=Nzc−qv=0.
