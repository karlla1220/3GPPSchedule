R1-2607812 FL Summary #1: GNSS resilient NR-NTN operation Moderator (Thales)

Agreement:

For GNSS-resilient NR-NTN initial access based on the transmission of two PRACH sequences using the same existing PRACH format and different roots (Alt 1-4), RAN1 to down-select one of the following alternatives:

  • The transmission of the two PRACH sequences is performed in separate ROs in the time domain (TDM method). The transmission of the two PRACH sequences overlapping in time and frequency within the same RO (CDM method) is deprioritized in R20 NR NTN.

  • Note: The above is decided based on the evaluation assumptions (such as LOS channel and 3 km/h) and studies during the corresponding study phase.

  • FFS: Other details

R1-2607813 FL Summary #2: GNSS resilient NR-NTN operation Moderator (Thales)

Agreement:

For handling of TA over-compensation (i.e., the UE's signal arrives earlier than expected) in initial access, RAN1 to discuss and down-select one or more of the following options for the GNSS-resilient operation:

  • Option 1: Explicit Signed TAC: Introduce a signed TAC field in the MAC RAR to directly indicate positive or negative timing corrections relative to the UE's open-loop pre-compensated transmission.

  • Option 2: TA Offset: Introduce a higher-layer configured TA offset. The UE applies this offset to all UL transmissions to bias its timing, ensuring that any subsequent TAC received in Msg2 is non-negative.

    • FFS: Granularity and value range for TA Offset

    • FFS: Whether TA Offset could be predefined

Agreement:

For handling of TA over-compensation (i.e., the UE's signal arrives earlier than expected) in initial access, support the following:

  • Negative/positive TAC: Introduce a TAC field in the MAC RAR to directly indicate positive or negative timing corrections relative to the UE's open-loop pre-compensated transmission.

  • TA Offset: Introduce a higher-layer configured TA offset. The UE applies this offset to all UL transmissions to bias its timing,

    • FFS: Granularity and value range for TA Offset

    • FFS: Whether TA Offset could be predefined

    • FFS: Whether TA Offset is configured per beam or cell

    • The higher layer TA offset can be configured to avoid any subsequent TAC received in Msg2 is negative
