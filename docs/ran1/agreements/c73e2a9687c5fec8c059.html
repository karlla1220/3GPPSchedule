  • For TRP monostatic sensing, {Delay/range, 3D angle} in C4 and Position in C5 can be mutually transformed, assuming the necessary assistance information, e.g., TRP location, and antenna orientation (LCS to GCS transformation) are available. Doppler and radial velocity can be mutually transformed, assuming carrier frequency is known.

  • Difference on confidence/uncertainty metrics of C4 and C5 may exist, depending on how they are indicated. For C4, uncertainty is indicated separately for the reported delay and angle metrics, thereby allowing different accuracies in the radial and angular dimensions to be represented. For C5, uncertainty is indicated in position domain through position uncertainty elipsoid. For C5, for example, additional rotation of the uncertainty ellipsoid to the radial direction of the target can be used to approximate the uncertainty result of C4.

  • The impact of confidence/uncertainty metric of C4 and C5 was not evaluated.
