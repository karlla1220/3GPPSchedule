Agreement

For 6GR multiple carrier operation in connected mode, for mechanisms for resource determination of a CORESET:

  • At least support a CORESET confined within a physical carrier as baseline

  • Whether to support a CORESET over multiple physical carriers is further discussed

    • FFS details for applicable scenario and condition, e.g., where Method 1 is supported, a carrier too narrow for the required aggregation level, etc

    • FFS Coordination with other WG(s), e.g., RAN4 (as needed)

Agreement

For bandwidth operation with single carrier operation in CONNECTED mode for a UE in 6G,

  • For DL, conclude the following for PDSCH and PDCCH reception

    • The schedulable frequency resources for the PDSCH is either identical to or contained within active “6GR-BW”.

    • PDCCH monitoring is within an active 6GR-BW.

  • Note: Handling of random access in CONNECTED mode to be revisited after random access in IDLE mode has progressed further under the initial access agenda item

Agreement

For mechanisms for PXSCH resource allocation in 6GR multiple carrier operation in connected mode, conclude the following:

  • Support a PXSCH confined within a physical carrier

Agreement

For mechanisms for PXSCH resource allocation in 6GR multiple carrier operation in connected mode, conclude

  • that Rel-20 SI prioritizes the study of single PDSCH over non-contiguous multiple carriers

  • FFS: single PDSCH over contiguous multiple carriers

Agreement

For fast enabling/disabling access to additional carriers for 6GR multiple carrier operation in connected mode, further discuss how to achieve fast enabling access to an additional carrier, considering the following:

  • For the study, RAN1 assumes the enabling operation is composed of one or more of the following:

    • Enabling command processing

    • RF preparation, e.g., retuning or switching on an RF module

    • Synchronization acquisition, TA and AGC

    • CSI acquisition and reporting

    • Others

  • Which of the above steps can be prepared or skipped, shortened before/after the command for enabling, depending on at least one or more of the following:

    • UE behaviors in an already-enabled carrier and another to-be-enabled carrier

    • The synchronization relationship between a to-be-enabled carrier and another already-enabled carrier, e.g., D1/D2/D3

    • Whether the to-be-enabled carrier shares an RF chain with the already-enabled carrier in UE side

    • The information kept by the UE for the to-be-enabled carrier in the disabled state, e.g., configuration, measurement, synchronization, CSI

  • From RAN1’s study, for synchronization acquisition/AGC with synchronization source on the to-be-enabled carrier, at least consider the following candidates:

    • SSB

    • OD-SSB

    • SS-only

    • Periodic TRS

    • Aperiodic TRS

    • Others
