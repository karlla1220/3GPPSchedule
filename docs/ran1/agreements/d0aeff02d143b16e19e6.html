Agreement

Support at least the following combinations of comb value and cyclic shift for comb values less than or equal to 8 (assuming the minimum bandwidth = 4 PRBs for SRS in 6GR)

  • The maximum number of cyclic shifts up to 24 for comb-2

  • The maximum number of cyclic shifts up to 12 for comb-4

  • The maximum number of cyclic shifts up to 6 for comb-8

Agreement

Support SRS repetition in 6GR.

Agreement

For DL CBW larger than 200MHz, for sounding of all parts of the target bandwidth over multiple transmissions of SRS with xTyR configration, support the following scheme

  • Spatial domain-first sounding: UE first sounds the y Rx ports within a part of DL BW with size of up to 200MHz, then UE RF-retune to the other part(s) of DL BW with size of up to 200MHz and sounds the y Rx ports

FFS: whether the different parts are non-overlapping, and whether they span the entire UL CBW

Note: the choice between single SRS resource and multiple SRS resources as per the RAN1#125 agreement is a separate discussion

Agreement

Support at least the following case of sharing one SRS transmission occasion for multiple usages:

  • One SRS transmission occasion for codebook-based UL transmission and for antenna switching

FFS: How to obtain CSI for both usages using a same SRS transmission occasion when the Tx port(s) number for UL CSI measurement is different from Rx port(s) number for DL CSI measurement.

FFS: Potential impact on spec, if any, to be considered in the WI. e.g., single or multiple resource sets

FFS: other usages that can be shared

Note: discuss separately on whether/how an SRS transmission occasion for non-CB-based UL transmission, if supported, and for antenna switching can be shared

Agreement

For 6GR, support at least the following interference randomization mechanisms

  • Mechanisms achieving sequence randomization

  • Comb offset hopping

  • Cyclic shift hopping

  • FFS: TD-OCC hopping (if TD-OCC is supported)

Agreement

Support dynamic adaptation (other than RRC configuration) of at least one of the following parameters of SRS:

  • SRS bandwidth and position in frequency domain

  • FH parameters

  • Number of SRS symbols, repetition factor and location in time domain

  • Slot offset

  • SRS periodicity

  • Comb offset and cyclic shift

  • Number of SRS ports

FFS: The time scale, applicable triggering timeline and applicable time behavior (i.e., P/AP) of dynamic adaptation for each of the above parameters.

FFS: Mechanism and constraints to enable dynamic adaptation of SRS parameters and associated signaling overhead

FFS: Dynamic adaptation of other parameters

Note: The benefit of the dynamic adaptation of each parameter, should be justified before it is supported.

Note: minimize the impact on other UL signals/channels

Agreement

For 2-level SRS frequency hopping patterns for 6GR, support the following pattern within frequency hopping period (Option 1 + Option 2):

  • First level: Hopping over the whole BW with one or multiple frequency hops following nested tree-like frequency hopping pattern as in NR, and

  • Second level: SRS within each frequency hop follows the partial frequency sounding.

    • Support fixed hopping patterns in the second level.

    • Details FFS. At least consider intra-repetition hopping specified in R20.

Note: The first level of 2-level hopping pattern can serve as the singlelevel SRS frequency hopping pattern

Agreement

For 6GR SRS, support start RB hopping across different frequency hopping periods.

Agreement

Send an LS to RAN4 to clarify the UE RF retuning/switching timeline and requirement when switching SRS between different frequency chunks under DL/UL CBW asymmetry, and inform RAN4 the agreements about SRS.

Agreement

Consider computer-generated SRS sequences for lengths < M, and down-select one option for the value of M:

  • Option-1: M = 30

  • Option-2: M>30 and<=251, with the exact value to be further studied.
