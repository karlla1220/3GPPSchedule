Agreement:

Cross-Ambiguity Peak (CAP) and Integrated Cross Ambiguity (ICA) are defined as follows.

  • Option 1

    • CA power is defined in delay-Doppler domain PCA(τ,ν)=|χCA(τ,ν)|2

    • Cross-Ambiguity Peak (CAP) = max((|χCA(τ,ν)|2))/sqrt(Px(0,0)∙Py(0,0)) over the (τ,ν) region

    • Integrated Cross Ambiguity (ICA) = ∬|χCA(τ,ν)|2dτdν/sqrt(Px(0,0)∙Py(0,0))over the (τ,ν) region

  • Option 2

    • CA power is defined in delay domain with zero or non-zero Doppler cut PCA(τ)=|χCA(τ)|2

    • Cross-Ambiguity Peak (CAP) = max((|χCA(τ)|2))/sqrt(Px(0,0)∙Py(0,0)) over the (τ) region

    • Integrated Cross Ambiguity (ICA) = [Equation: χCAτ2dτsqrt(Px0,0∙Py0,0)] over the τ region

Note: companies should report details of entire/interested (or a part of) delay-Doppler region to calculate CAP and ICA

Agreement:

For effective CP length extension based on CP-OFDM enhancement, if the necessity and benefits are identified, the following methods can be considered for further study for handling the sensing signal reception beyond CP length in 6G ISAC.

  • Based on at least N=2 successive symbols

    • N successive symbols with phase continuity across symbols based on TD cyclic shift or FD phase rotation of sensing symbol(s)

    • N-times CP length + N repetition of OFDM symbols without CP

  • Based on comb size of at least {2, 4} in frequency domain

  • Based on time gap before/after/within a sensing symbol

    • Symbol-level time gap before and/or after sensing symbol

    • Combination of a gap and a pulse-like signal within a symbol

  • Implementation-based approaches such as based on window processing and/or sync processing at receiver and/or CP length-based range of interest for target(s) can also be considered.

R1-2610358 Summary #4 on waveform and frame structure for 6G ISAC Moderator (LGE)

Agreement:

Previous agreement on PAPR definition is updated as follows by resolving FFS points.

Agreement:

PAPR is defined as one of the following options.

  • PAPR is defined as per-symbol Iinstantaneous PAPR, which is defined per OFDM symbol at [0.1 and/or 1 %] CCDF of the instantaneous sample power-to-the average sample power within OFDM symbol, with the IFFT oversampling factor (≥4) reported by companies

  • PAPR CCDF over the RS sequence family

    • PAPRMean, variation and the best, worst PAPR of sensing symbols within CPI is reported

    • FFS: per-sequence and worst case PAPR when [1%] cannot be met (e.g. 60 NR ZC sequences)

    • FFS: PAPR for pulse-like waveform, both (PAPR within symbol duration or and PAPR within pulse duration) are reported

    • Note: how to determine Tx power gain for pulse-like signal is separately discussed

Note: Tx-side processing assumption including frequency domain processing/A(m)/time-domain clipping is reported together with PAPR.

Agreement:

Companies are encouraged to report details of multiplexing capacity of waveform/RS sequences i.e., the number of different RS sequences.
