R1-2608606 FL summary #4 for AI 10.5.4.3: 6GR HARQ related aspects Moderator (OPPO)

Conclusion:

Adopt the following TP for TR 38.760-1 Clause 5.6.3.

For DL HARQ in 6GR, support HARQ-ACK information bits transmitted via L1 signalling (i.e., Mechanism 1).

  • The design of DL HARQ-ACK feedback operation allows at least the possibility for the following while ensuring HARQ-ACK performance

    • Relaxing the need for tight coordination between schedulers (e.g., between multiple carriers, between DL and UL, etc.)

      • Decoupling of HARQ-ACK feedback triggering from the DL assignments scheduling the corresponding PDSCH

      • Other solution that addresses the same issue can also be considered

    • Other factors can also be considered

  • The design of DL HARQ-ACK feedback operation allows the simplification of UL channel collision/multiplexing/transmission (e.g., resource determination) involving HARQ-ACK

Agreement:

In 6GR, study how to determine minimum UE processing timelines at least for following:

  • from end of PDSCH reception to channel carrying corresponding HARQ-ACK (e.g., NR N1)

    • Note, in NR the channel carrying corresponding HARQ-ACK is PUCCH

  • from end of PDCCH reception to corresponding PUSCH (e.g., NR N2)

  • from end of PDCCH reception without PDSCH scheduling to channel carrying corresponding HARQ-ACK (e.g., NR N in TS 38.213 Clause 10.2)

    • Note, in NR the channel carrying corresponding HARQ-ACK is PUCCH

  • FFS: whether more than one UE capability is needed (e.g., two capabilities in NR)

  • FFS: impact of energy efficiency operation on the processing timelines, if any, including impact to NW complexity, if any

  • FFS: impact of large TBS on the UE processing timelines, if any

Agreement:

In 6GR, study the following two cases and potential factors to determine the number of DL and UL HARQ processes at least per cell/carrier.

  • Case 1: the minimum number of HARQ processes at least for TN to be supported by a UE per cell/carrier

  • Case 2: the maximum number of HARQ processes at least for TN supported per cell/carrier supported by the specification

  • Potential factors include at least the following:

    • Frequency range(s) / SCS

    • HARQ RTT for single-carrier and multi-carrier operations (processing/re-scheduling timeline, TDD pattern, duplex mode, number of PUCCH group)

    • UE complexity, considering peak data rate and soft-buffer memory (e.g., bandwidth, layers, modulation order)

    • Network complexity impact, performance impact and deployment flexibility

  • FFS: whether / how to limit the number of HARQ processes across cells/carriers

  • Note: the above does not have implication on the UE capability reporting framework

Proposal 4-2/4-3 (II):

In Method 1, study the avoidance of PUCCH resource overriding (when applicable) and the decoupling of HARQ-ACK feedback triggering from the DL assignment(s) scheduling the corresponding PDSCH(s) (when applicable) at least based on the following framework:

  • A DL DCI with/without scheduling corresponding PDSCH(s) with associated HARQ-ACK feedback can indicate applicable HARQ-ACK feedback timing and resource.

    • FFS: whether/how to allow/avoid changing the HARQ-ACK feedback resource by later DL DCI(s)

  • A DL DCI with/without scheduling corresponding PDSCH(s) with associated HARQ-ACK feedback can indicate e.g., via invalid/absent timing and/or resource or other indication that corresponding HARQ-ACK feedback is not triggered, and the corresponding HARQ-ACK feedback is not transmitted until a subsequent DCI indicates the timing and/or resource for HARQ-ACK feedback

    • The subsequent DCI can be a DL DCI with/without scheduling another PDSCH, an UL DCI scheduling a PUSCH carrying the HARQ-ACK feedback, or a dedicated DCI triggering the HARQ-ACK feedback

    • once the HARQ-ACK feedback resource is indicated by the subsequent DCI, it cannot be changed by a later DL DCI from UE perspective

    • FFS: once the HARQ-ACK feedback resource is indicated by the subsequent DCI which is scheduling DCI, whether/how to allow/avoid changing the HARQ-ACK feedback resource by later DL DCI(s)

    • FFS: how the UE and NW identify which PDSCH(s)/HARQ-ACK bits are associated with or resolved by the subsequent DCI.

  • FFS: further details (e.g., timing indication in case of multiple PDSCH scheduling, and how the HARQ-ACK feedback timing is defined, resolution for the last DCI missing)
