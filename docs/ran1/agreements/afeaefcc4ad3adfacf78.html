  • FFS: metric of the power if applicable

  • Note: applicability to sensing mode/entity can be discussed separately

[FL1][H] Proposal 6.2-1-rev1

  • For profile-based measurement, study followings

    • One or multiple windows of consecutive samples applies in delay-Doppler-angle profile

      • Note: one or multiple dimensions can be disabled

        • FFS: details on disabling

    • Note: Selection of the window(s) by the sensing receiver and/or configuration by the network is up to normative phase discussion.

    • It is not precluded by configuration that the window can cover the whole range in one or more profiles

  • The following solutions for further overhead reduction for profile-based measurement can be considered for further study including the necessity of further overhead reduction

    • Reporting top N strongest samples in the window(s)

    • Reporting samples with power above threshold

    • Differential report of samples across time

    • Quantization schemes

    • Event-triggered reporting

    • Other solutions are not precluded
