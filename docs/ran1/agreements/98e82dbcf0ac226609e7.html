[126bis-R20-6GR-MC-Operation in connected mode] Email discussion on Rel-20 6GR-MC-Operation in connected mode –Naoya (NTT DOCOMO, INC.)

  • To be used for sharing updates on online/offline schedule, details on what is to be discussed in online/offline sessions, tdoc number of the moderator summary for online session, etc

For study of flexible utilization of spectrum resources for DL and UL for connected mode over different carriers/bands, considering at least the following:

  • Applicable scenario(s) and condition(s), e.g.,

    • Applicable collocation conditions, including collocated and/or non-collocated

    • Applicable timing/frequency synchronization relationship, e.g., D1/D2/D3 as agreed in RAN1#126 under 10.4.1

  • Detailed study objectives in respective scenario(s) and condition(s), e.g.,

    • Synchronization acquisition

    • TA determination

    • Spatial filter determination

    • UL Tx power control (e.g., pathloss calculation/estimation)

    • Under which scenario(s)/condition(s) concurrent UL transmission and/or DL reception across the considered multiple carriers are allowed or not

    • Whether/which scenario(s)/condition(s) concurrent DL reception across the considered multiple carriers are allowed or not

    • Under which scenario(s)/condition(s) UL Tx switching across multiple carriers are allowed or not

    • Whether/which scenario(s)/condition(s) DL Rx switching (considering usecase to be identified) across multiple carriers are allowed or not

    • Applicable DL/UL signals/channels

    • Scheduling carrier(s) for the UL/DL carrier(s)

    • How different UL/DL carriers are associated with different or same cell(s)

    • Whether the paired UL carrier(s) are UL resources of a serving cell (as the SUL in NR) or separate cell(s)

    • Other objectives not precluded

  • Detailed cases, e.g.,:

    • Case 1: 1 DL carrier and 1 UL carrier, where the DL and UL carrier are frequency separated, and no other DL carrier or UL carrier is configured for a UE

    • Case 2: 1 DL carrier and N UL carriers (N>1), where at least 1 UL carrier is frequency separated from the DL carrier, and no other DL carrier is configured for a UE

    • Case 3: M DL carriers (M>1) and 1 UL carrier, where at least 1 DL carrier is frequency separated from the UL carrier, and no other UL carrier is configured for a UE

    • Case 4: M DL carriers (M>1) and N UL carriers (N>1), where at least one of the following conditions is met:

      • At least 1 DL carrier is frequency separated from all the UL carriers

      • At least 1 UL carrier is frequency separated from all the DL carriers

    • Case 5: M DL carriers (M>1) and N UL carriers (N>1), where all the following conditions are met

      • For each DL carrier, at least one of UL carriers is not frequency separated from the DL carrier

      • For each UL carrier, at least one of DL carriers is not frequency separated from the UL carrier

      • At least one DL carrier is frequency separated from at least one UL carrier

    • Note: For above cases, whether it can be a behavior in 6GR multiple carrier operation (e.g., Method 1, Method 2, transmission/reception switching across carriers) or not is also studied

    • FFS possible frequency separation in each of the cases from RAN1 point of view

      • Note: For the discussion purpose, in FDD and TDD in 5G NR, DL carrier and UL carrier are regarded not frequency separated
