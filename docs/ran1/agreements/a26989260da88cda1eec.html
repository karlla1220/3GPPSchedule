Observation:

Up to RAN1#127, for 6G ISAC performance evaluation of sensing assisted communication, for SAC-Scope 1, for evaluation of channel properties of delay/Doppler/angle of selected path(s), 11 results from 7 sources (vivo, CATT/CICTCI, Huawei/Hisilicon, Ericsson, Lenovo, InterDigital, AT&T) are reported in R1-2609701. The following is observed based on the submitted results.

The following performances are achieved:

  • RMSE of delay of selected path(s): 0.19 to 14.9 ns

  • RMSE of Doppler of selected path(s): 0 to 0.5925 Hz

  • RMSE of AOD of selected path(s): 0.79 to 6.32 degree

  • RMSE of ZOD of selected path(s): 0.10 to 5.31 degree

  • RMSE of AOA of selected path(s): 0.0372 to 2.6 degree

  • RMSE of ZOA of selected path(s): 0 to 6.6 degree

Based on the following assumptions:

  • Sensing mode: TRP mono (1 source), TRP-TRP bi (1 source), TRP-UE bi (5 sources), UE-TRP bi (1 source)

  • Overhead 1: 0% to 9.14%

  • Overhead 2: 0% to 9.14%

  • CPI length: 20 to 800 ms

  • STx/SRx beam (Mt*Nt) x (Mr*Mr): Single beam and multiple beams (up to 3*1 for STx and 1*1 for SRx)

  • Inter-site interference modelling: Not modelled

  • Inter-sector interference modelling: Not modelled
