For UL transmission switching across carriers in 6GR, RAN1 recommends supporting UL transmission switching across carriers in Rel-21 6GR, assuming the following:

  • A UL Tx chain switch can be applied at least for either PUSCH or SRS

    • FFS details, e.g.,

      • Whether/how the switch applies to PUCCH/PRACH, CG-PUSCH

        • Note: SRS antenna switching behavior in a carrier before/after the UL transmission switching is to be addressed in other agenda

        • Note: It does not imply that the same Tx chains are used by UEs for PUCCH/PRACH transmissions as PUSCH transmissions

  • How to support switching of carrier for UL transmission is addressed in WI, at least considering the following:

    • SRS carrier switching, including whether/how to achieve common/similar design to UL Tx switch above

    • Intra-band PUSCH carrier switching, including whether/how to achieve common/similar design to UL Tx switch above

  • Signaling to trigger UL transmission switching is to be addressed in WI phase, considering semi-static and/or dynamic manner

Send LS to RAN4 informing this agreement.

Agreement

For 6GR multiple carrier operation in connected mode, for mechanisms for resource determination of a CORESET:

  • At least support a CORESET confined within a physical carrier as baseline

  • Whether to support a CORESET over multiple physical carriers is further discussed

    • FFS details for applicable scenario and condition, e.g., where Method 1 is supported, a carrier too narrow for the required aggregation level, etc

    • FFS Coordination with other WG(s), e.g., RAN4 (as needed)

For scheduling for 6G multiple carrier operation,

  • Support self-carrier scheduling

  • Support cross-carrier UL scheduling

    • Further details/conditions for the above to be addressed in WI phase

  • FFS multi-carrier scheduling

  • FFS cross-carrier DL scheduling

Agreement

For bandwidth operation with single carrier operation in CONNECTED mode for a UE in 6G,

  • For DL, conclude the following for PDSCH and PDCCH reception

    • The schedulable frequency resources for the PDSCH is either identical to or contained within active “6GR-BW”.

    • PDCCH monitoring is within an active 6GR-BW.

  • Note: Handling of random access in CONNECTED mode to be revisited after random access in IDLE mode has progressed further under the initial access agenda item
