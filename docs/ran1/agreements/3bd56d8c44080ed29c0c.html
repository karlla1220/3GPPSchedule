Agreement

For multiple carriers where, for initial access, a UE can utilize resources for UL (Msg1, etc.) on a carrier other than the carrier on which the UE obtains the system information providing the configuration for those resources, support at least S1 with D1.

  • S1: 1 DL physical carrier + N UL physical carriers, where N > 1

  • Scenarios in which a UE assumes co-located carriers with a common timing/frequency synchronization relationship can be assumed

  • Applicable at least to UC-1 (UL coverage enhancement / DL-UL coverage imbalance)

  • FFS: Applicability to other use case(s)

  • FFS the maximum number of UL physical carriers N

  • At least one of the N UL carriers is in the same band as the DL carrier

  • Note: under certains deployments limits, a solution developped for supporting S1 under D1 may also allow handling S1 under D2 or D3 by network implementation.

Agreement

For multiple carriers where, for initial access, a UE can utilize resources for UL (Msg1, etc.) on a carrier other than the carrier on which the UE obtains the system information providing the configuration for those resources, support at least S1 with D1.

  • S1: 1 DL physical carrier + N UL physical carriers, where N > 1

  • D1: co-located carriers for which a common timing/frequency synchronization relationship can be assumed

    • Note: common timing/frequency synchronization relationship are assumed from UE perspective

  • Applicable at least to UC-1 (UL coverage enhancement / DL-UL coverage imbalance)

  • FFS: Applicability to other use case(s)

  • FFS the maximum number of UL physical carriers N

  • At least one of the N UL carriers is in the same band as the DL carrier

Agreement

For further study of initial access for idle mode multi-carrier operation, consider the following additional potential use case:

  • UC-4: Direct access to a wider-bandwidth carrier

    • In this use case, an idle/inactive UE camps on a lower-frequency coverage carrier (e.g., FDD) for SI and paging. When [a service requires wider bandwidth and] the UE is within coverage of a wider-bandwidth capacity carrier (e.g., TDD), the UE can use the configuration obtained on the camping carrier to perform initial access directly on the wider-bandwidth carrier.

    • Study UC-4 under S3b

    • Compare direct initial access on the wider-bandwidth carrier with the following reference procedures.

      • Single-carrier initial access on the camping carrier followed by handover to the capacity carrier

      • Single-carrier initial access on the camping carrier followed by addition/activation of the capacity carrier as an SCell

      • Cell reselection to the wider-bandwidth carrier followed by single-carrier initial access on that carrier

      • Cell camping on the wider-bandwidth carrier followed by single-carrier initial access on that carrier

    • Compare direct initial access on the capacity carrier with each reference procedure in terms of the following aspects:

      • UE synchronization and measurement effort, including at least the time required and the associated UE and NW energy consumption, and failure cases

        • Cooperation with RAN2 and RAN4

Agreement

For S1 with D1 assuming UC-1, consider the following aspects for initial access:

  • UL access carrier selection

    • Take measurement-based selection as the starting point. The UE selects the UL carrier for Msg1 based on measurements on the SI-providing DL carrier.

      • FFS details including measurement during WI

      • FFS in case the maximum number of UL physical carriers N>2

    • FFS: Other candidate options

  • Reference signal-to-RO mapping

  • UL Tx power control

    • Use SSBs on the SI-providing carrier for pathloss estimation.

    • FFS how to account for the pathloss difference between that DL carrier and a candidate UL carrier during WI

    • Note: other reference signal can be considered if additional reference signal to SSB is supported

Agreement

For study of S3a, consider the following aspects for initial access, and decide whether to support S3a and the applicable deployment condition(s) in RAN1#128

  • Applicable at least to UC-1 (UL coverage enhancement / DL-UL coverage imbalance)

  • FFS: Applicability to other use case(s)

  • Consider D2 and D3 for study

  • UL access carrier selection

    • For UC-1, take measurement-based selection as the starting point. Study the necessity and conditions of UL access carrier selection based on measurements on the non-SI-providing carrier

  • Reference signal-to-RO mapping

    • Study the necessity and conditions of RO mapping from the SSBs on the non-SI-providing carrier

  • UL Tx power control

    • Study the necessity and conditions of pathloss estimation based on SSBs on the non-SI-providing carrier

  • Synchronization acquisition, UL transmission timing

    • Study the necessity and conditions of synchronization acquisition and/or UL transmission timing based on SSBs on the non-SI-providing carrier

  • Note: other reference signal can be considered if additional reference signal to SSB is supported

Conclusion

For the purpose of RAN1 discussion, for S1 and S3 (S3a and S3b), each of N UL physical carriers can be from a FDD band, a TDD band or an SUL band as defined in 5G NR.

  • Note: band definition and evaluation of RF on UE is up to RAN4.
