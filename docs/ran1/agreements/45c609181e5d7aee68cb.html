R1-2607230 Summary #1 on integration with communication for ISAC Moderator (Xiaomi)

Agreement:

  • The following interference types need to be studied for 6G ISAC, which are dependent on sensing modes and sensing scenarios.

    • Self-interference (for the case where mono-static is involved)

    • The interference between sensing signals

    • The interference between sensing and communication

  • Companies are encouraged to bring up evaluation results on potential interference issues.

    • E.g., what is interference source, in which sensing mode, performance difference with/without interference are recommended for the study.

  • If interference issue for sensing needs to be resolved, the following aspects of interference control can be studied in Rel-20, taking interference handling for communication as starting point.

    • Coordinated resource allocation in time/frequency/code/spatial domain, including e.g., measurement/transmission gap/window

    • Resource muting

    • Rate matching

    • Power control

    • Interference resource/measurement/report

    • Implementation method

    • Other aspects are not precluded

Agreement:

  • Study at least the following beam related aspects for Rel-20 ISAC taking communication beam management as starting point, to identify whether sensing specific enhancement on beam related aspects for sensing is necessary or not

    • Measurement/report/information to determine best Tx-Rx beam pair(s) for sensing

    • QCL/TCI state or spatial-relation assumptions of sensing RS

R1-2607231 Summary #2 on integration with communication for ISAC Moderator (Xiaomi)

Agreement:

  • Study at least the following aspects of open loop power control of UE as sensing transmitter using existing SRS power control framework as starting point, to identify whether sensing specific enhancement on power control for sensing is necessary or not

    • P0/alpha

    • PCMAX

    • Power boosting

    • Pathloss/Pathloss reference

    • PH/PHR

    • Association of the applicable power-control parameters for sensing purpose

    • UE transmission power within CPI

    • the impact/interference to communication and sensing

  • Companies are encouraged to bring up evaluation results on potential power control issues.

Working assumption:

  • RAN1 adopts the following definition on nominal Coherent Processing Interval (CPI) for discussion purpose

    • “nominal CPI”: The time interval for sensing measurements during which the sensing RS resource can be coherently processed unless intolerable phase discontinuity and/or power inconsistency happens.

      • FFS: in nominal CPI, the sensing RS resource can be coherently processed in time domain only or in other domain as well

      • FFS: whether/how both transmitter and receiver have information on nominal CPI

      • FFS: solutions to maintain/recover the phase continuity and/or power consistency in a nominal CPI

      • FFS: details on intolerable phase discontinuity and/or power inconsistency

    • Note: the use nominal CPI in RAN1 discussion doesn’t necessarily mean such terms will be specified in normative phase
