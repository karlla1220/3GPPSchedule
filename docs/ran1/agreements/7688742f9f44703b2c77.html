Adopt the following TP to section 5.1.5 TS 38.214 in principle:

Reason for change:

When the unified TCI framework is configured, WUS in RRC_CONNECTED follows the indicated TCI state, the same as other DL channels/signals (RAN1#120bis agreement; TS 38.213 clause 10.4D).

Before application of an indicated TCI state, TS 38.214 clause 5.1.5 specifies the QCL assumption of WUS for an LTM cell switch by the LTM Cell Switch Command MAC CE and for a Reconfiguration with sync procedure (based on the SS/PBCH block identified during the random access procedure), and otherwise the SS/PBCH block identified during the initial access procedure. For RACH-less conditional LTM cell switch, the clause specifies the determined CandidateTCI-State only for DM-RS of PDSCH and PDCCH and CSI-RS.

After a RACH-less conditional LTM cell switch, there is neither an LTM Cell Switch Command MAC CE nor a random access procedure. WUS therefore falls under the 'otherwise' case and is assumed quasi co-located with the SS/PBCH block of the initial access procedure, which may belong to a different cell, while the other DL channels/signals on the target cell use the determined CandidateTCI-State.

TS 38.214 clause 5.1.5 specifies an explicit RACH-based conditional LTM cell switch case for DM-RS of PDSCH and PDCCH and CSI-RS, but not for WUS.

Summary of change:

Add the QCL assumption for WUS for RACH-less conditional LTM cell switch (the reference signal in the determined CandidateTCI-State) and for RACH-based conditional LTM cell switch (the SS/PBCH block identified during the random access procedure), with respect to 'typeC', and when applicable, also with respect to 'typeD', consistent with DM-RS of PDSCH and PDCCH and CSI-RS.

Consequences if not approved:

After a RACH-less conditional LTM cell switch, the UE assumes that WUS is quasi co-located with the SS/PBCH block identified during the initial access procedure, which may belong to a different cell, and may not receive WUS on the target cell; the QCL assumption for WUS after a RACH-based conditional LTM cell switch is not specified consistently with the other DL channels/signals.
