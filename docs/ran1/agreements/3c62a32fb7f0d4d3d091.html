R1-2607824 FL summary #1 for 10.5.4.2 DL/UL transmission without dynamic scheduling Moderator(1Finity)

Agreement:

DCI-based dynamic activation is at least supported in 6GR for a CG-PUSCH configuration which is not immediately activated upon the configuration.

Agreement:

Study UL skipping for DG/CG-PUSCH in 6GR.

  • Note: coordination with RAN2 is necessary for this study.

R1-2607825 FL summary #2 for 10.5.4.2 DL/UL transmission without dynamic scheduling Moderator(1Finity)

R1-2607826 FL summary #3 for 10.5.4.2 DL/UL transmission without dynamic scheduling Moderator(1Finity)

Agreement:

For dynamic adjustment of parameters at least for SPS-PDSCH configurations and CG-PUSCH configurations that are not immediately activated and are subsequently activated by an activation DCI, study which parameter(s) can be or cannot be dynamically adjusted and its use-cases/benefits.

  • The parameters that could be dynamically adjusted via activation DCI for SPS-PDSCH and Type 2 CG-PUSCH in 5G NR is considered as starting point.

    • Note: The set of parameters to be indicated by activation DCI in 6GR is subject to separate discussion.

Conclusion:

Activation of SPS-PDSCH configurations through RRC signaling is not supported in 6GR.

Agreement:

At least for a CG-PUSCH configuration which is not immediately activated and subsequently activated by activation DCI,

  • Support DCI-based deactivation of the configuration

  • FFS: other signaling
