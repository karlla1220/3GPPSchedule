R1-2608574 FL summary#1 on handling of potential multiple uplink transmissions Moderator (MediaTek)

Agreement:

For 6GR, study the following methods to simplify the collision-handling procedures for UL channels/signals/UCI:

  • “One shot” collision-handling based on a time unit (e.g., slot) to avoid iterative process

    • FFS the steps for the collision handling for the “one-shot” collision handling procedure.

    • FFS channels/signals/UCI involved in the “one-shot” collision handling procedure for a given time unit

  • Defining a single deterministic reference point for a given time unit for the timeline for processing collision-handling.

    • FFS the actual reference point

  • Defining a deterministic timeline for processing collision-handling

    • FFS the actual value(s) of timeline for processing collision-handling

  • Note: the reference point and timeline in the above are at least applicable to the scheduling/triggering PDCCH

    • FFS: applicability to other channels/signals

R1-2608575 FL summary#2 on handling of potential multiple uplink transmissions Moderator (MediaTek)

Agreement:

For the collision handling between PUCCH and PUSCH on different carriers:

  • For a UE supporting inter-band CA on a band combination, support simultaneous PUSCH and PUCCH transmissions on different carriers in an inter-band CA configuration of the band combination

    • Note: RAN4 has agreed “6G FR1 inter-band UL CA power limit will not be constrained by the combined UL band combination power class as in 5G, i.e. PPowerClass,CA specified in clause 6.2A.4.1.3 of TS 38.101-1 will be removed in 6G. Instead, the UE indicates a per-band power class. Each band in the UL configuration (either single carrier or contiguous UL CA), may transmit up to the maximum output power in accordance with the indicated power class for that band.”

  • Study mechanisms for intra-band CA scenario:

    • Intra-band contiguous CA

    • Intra-band non-contiguous CA

  • Study mechanisms for UL Tx switching scenario.

Agreement:

For the study of “one shot” collision-handling based on a time-unit to avoid iterative process, the time-unit is one slot.

  • FFS: additional time-unit options and applicable scenarios

R1-2608576 FL summary#3 on handling of potential multiple uplink transmissions Moderator (MediaTek)

Agreement:

For the study of defining a single deterministic reference point for a given time-unit for the timeline for processing collision-handling, the reference point is the start of the time-unit.

  • FFS: additional options and applicable scenarios.

Agreement:

For the study of defining a deterministic timeline for processing collision-handling, study whether the timeline for processing collision-handling can be independent of the potential UL channels/signals/UCI scheduled/configured within the time-unit.

Agreement:

For 6GR, study the network signalling methods to simplify collision-handling procedures for UL channels/signals/UCI. Companies are encouraged to provide details on the methods in RAN1#127.

Note: other methods are not precluded.
