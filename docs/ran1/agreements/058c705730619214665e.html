Agreement:

For 6GR PUSCH transmission with CP-OFDM waveform, study DMRS sequence at least considering the followings:

  • Based on Gold sequence initialized by a scrambling ID

    • Consider how to facilitate PAPR reduction property

    • FFS: which modulation is used for DMRS, e.g., QPSK, 8PSK

    • FFS: number of scrambling ID(s)

  • Investigate possible further enhancement(s) considering the following directions

    • Whether/how to mitigate inter-port interference

    • Whether/how to mitigate inter-cell interference

  • Auto-/Cross-correlation property

  • PAPR, cubic metric

  • Consider possibly common design between PDSCH and PUSCH DMRS for CP-OFDM

  • Consider impact on receiver complexity

Agreement

For 6GR PUSCH transmission with DFTS-OFDM waveform, study DMRS sequence at least considering the followings:

  • ZC sequence and CGS in 5G NR as a starting point

  • Further considering enhancement(s) with the following directions

    • Whether the DMRS sequence should depend on the PUSCH modulation order

    • Whether/how to enhance ZC, Gold and CGS-based DMRS sequence design

    • Whether a new/refined low-PAPR DMRS sequence is introduced

    • Whether the DMRS sequence is independent of FDRA size/start for flexible MU-MIMO pairing

    • Whether/how to mitigate inter-port interference

    • Whether/how to mitigate inter-cell interference

  • Auto-/Cross-correlation property including cross-correlation with partially overlapping sequences

  • PAPR, cubic metric

  • Channel estimation accuracy

  • Note: DMRS sequence for the case of DMRS RE and PUSCH data RE in a same symbol by FDM manner can be separately discussed.

  • Consider possibly common design between PUSCH DMRS for DFTS-OFDM modulated by QPSK and above and SRS

    • FFS: PUSCH DMRS for DFTS-OFDM modulated by pi/2 BPSK

  • Consider impact on receiver complexity

Agreement

For 6GR PUSCH transmission with DFTS-OFDM waveform, study whether/how to map DMRS RE and PUSCH data RE in a same symbol by FDM manner, with considering at least the following.

  • Consider PAPR/CM property of DMRS symbol, and PUSCH performance

  • Receiver complexity

Agreement:

For 6GR PUSCH transmission, study PTRS design considering at least the followings:

  • Supported frequency range(s)

  • Maximum number of PTRS port(s)

  • Time/frequency RE mapping

  • Determination method of time/frequency density

  • PTRS-DMRS association

  • PTRS power boosting

  • Condition on presence of PT-RS

  • Potential use case(s) (e.g. Corresponding potential enhancement for cross-slot PUSCH if supported)

  • The phase noise model defined in TR38.803.

  • Scheme 1. CSI-RS based DL channel estimation by UE, UE calculates UL precoder, UE transmits (precoded) SRS, and NW indicates UE precoder information to schedule (e.g., analogous with non-codebook based PUSCH in NR)

  • Support a single PUSCH mapping type

  • For starting symbol S and number of symbols L, support S among {0,1,….,13}, L among {1,2,…,14}

    • L=1 is supported for CP-OFDM

    • FFS: L=1 for DFTS-OFDM depending on the discussion of mapping DMRS RE and PUSCH data RE in a same symbol by FDM manner

  • Note: DMRS time domain location is discussed separately.

Agreement:

For time domain resource allocation for PUSCH across more than one slot in 6GR, at least support single TRP-based PUSCH repetition across multiple slots on a per slot basis,

  • With the same starting symbol and length per slot.

  • FFS: Whether/how to additionally support different starting symbol and/or different length per slot.

Agreement:

For frequency domain resource allocation for a PUSCH on a single physical carrier in 6GR,

  • Support RIV-based (“Type-I”) allocation as a physically contiguous allocation method

  • Study whether/how to support RBG-bitmap-based (“Type-0”) allocation as a physically almost contiguous RB-level (as in 5G NR) allocation method for CP-OFDM

    • Companies are encouraged to submit analysis on use cases and simulation results until RAN1#127 for decision on whether it is supported

    • Considering impact on UL performance, MPR, scheduling restriction

  • Study whether/how to support RBG-bitmap-based (“Type-0”) allocation as a physically contiguous allocation method for CP-OFDM and DFTS-OFDM

Agreement:

For RIV-based (“Type-I”) frequency domain resource allocation for a PUSCH on a single physical carrier in 6GR,

  • Support the following unit as a granularity

    • 1 RB

    • FFS: Integer multiple of 1 RB

    • FFS: whether/how granularity is configurable
