R1-2607939 Feature Lead summary #1 on 6G waveform Moderator (Nokia)

Proposal

Update the agreement made at RAN1#125 as below.

  • FDSS with transparent filtering is recommended beneficial to be supported for π/2-BPSK from RAN1 perspective, based on RAN1 observations

    • FFS: QPSK, 16QAM

Feature lead proposal:

Discuss the following observations alternatives on FDSS

Alternative 1

Transparent FDSS compared with no spectrum shaping (use cases 1–4): For π/2-BPSK (two sources), FDSS provides a net gain of 0.8–1.6 dB (outer) and -0.3 dB to 0.9 dB (inner) at SE 0.23–0.31. For QPSK (four sources), most results are within -2.8 dB to 0.9 dB at SE 0.23–1.33, with a large spread across sources. For 16QAM (two sources), FDSS results in a net loss of 2.2–3.6 dB (inner) and 1.5–2.9 dB (outer) at SE 1.48–2.57. When CFR is applied to the reference scheme, the net gain is -0.2 dB to 0.2 dB for QPSK at SE 0.74–1.33 (single source) and -0.7 dB to 0.2 dB for 16QAM at SE 1.33–2.57 (single source, inner only).

Alternative 2

Compared with no spectrum shaping, transparent FDSS provides a net gain for π/2-BPSK, mainly for outer RB allocation. For QPSK, most results show a moderate net loss or a small net gain, with a large spread across sources. For 16QAM, most results show a net loss. When CFR is applied to the reference scheme, the net gain of FDSS for QPSK and 16QAM is within approximately ±0.7 dB.

-   FDSS gain for π/2-BPSK (Use case 1), reference scheme without CFR (two sources): a net gain of -0.3 dB to 0.9 dB (inner) and 0.8–1.6 dB (outer) at SE 0.23–0.31; one source reports a net gain of 3.6 dB at SE 0.54 (inner only). No results were provided with CFR applied to the reference scheme;

-   FDSS for QPSK (Use case 2), reference scheme without CFR (four sources): most results are within a net gain of -2.8 dB to 0.9 dB at SE 0.23–1.33, and outer RB allocation generally performs better than inner; one source reports net losses of up to 12.5 dB, and one source reports a net gain of 2.0 dB at SE 1.08 (inner only). With CFR applied to the reference scheme (single source), the net gain is -0.2 dB to -0.1 dB (inner) and 0.1–0.2 dB (outer) at SE 0.74–1.33;

-   FDSS for 16QAM (use case 3), reference scheme without CFR (two sources): a net loss of 2.2–3.6 dB (inner) and 1.5–2.9 dB (outer) at SE 1.48–2.57; one source reports a net gain of 0.2 dB at SE 2.16 (inner only). With CFR applied to the reference scheme (single source, inner only), the net gain is -0.7 dB to 0.2 dB at SE 1.33–2.57;
