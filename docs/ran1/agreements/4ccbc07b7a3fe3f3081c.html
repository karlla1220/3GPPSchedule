Conclusion:

For a CG-PUSCH configuration that is not immediately activated upon configuration and is subsequently activated by activation DCI:

  • No additional dynamic deactivation signaling other than DCI-based is supported under agenda item 10.5.4.2.

Observation:

  • UL skipping aims to avoid unnecessary UL transmissions, thereby reducing UE power consumption and potential UL interference.

  • Rel-15 and Rel-16 UL skipping behaviors regarding UCI multiplexing are different as follows:

    • In Rel-15, e.g., when there is no UL data or A-CSI to be transmitted on a PUSCH (and UL skipping configured), the PUSCH is skipped even when overlapping with PUCCH.

    • In Rel-16, when Rel-16 UL skipping is enabled and there is no UL data to be transmitted on a PUSCH while a UCI is to be multiplexed onto the PUSCH, the PUSCH is not skipped and is transmitted to carry the UCI.

Agreement:

  • Support UL skipping mechanism for DG-PUSCH and CG-PUSCH in 6GR, while the following aspects are subject to further discussion during the WI phase:

    • Detailed skipping rules for cases involving UCI multiplexing.

    • FFS: Others, including conditions for skipping the PUSCH and/or signaling for skipping at least for DG-PUSCH

  • Further study till RAN1#128,

    • Whether/how to solve the ambiguity at the base station side when UL-skipping is enabled and no PUSCH is received on the PUSCH resource,

      • FFS: the cases involved in the ambiguity

    • FFS: Partial skipping using a subset of PUSCH resources
