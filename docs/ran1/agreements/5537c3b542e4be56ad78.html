R1-2610257 Feature Lead summary #3 on PDCCH Transmission Schemes Moderator (Nokia)

Agreement:

On 6GR PDCCH REG-bundle size and precoding granularity, consider the following alternatives. Down select one alternative by RAN1#128:

  • Alt.1: Do not introduce neither REG bundles sizes larger than 6 REGs nor precoding granularity larger than a REG bundle

  • Alt.2: Only for REG bundle size of 6 REGs support the same precoder for multiple contiguous REG-bundles in time/frequency

    • Alt.2a: Support precoding granularity Option 3 or Option 4 (decide which one) with the number of continuous REG bundles with the same precoding granularity of either 2 or 4 (decide which one).

    • Alt.2b: Support precoding granularity Option 2 (wideband precoding)

  • Alt.3: Introduce REG bundle size of either 12 or 24 REGs (decide which one) and do not support precoding granularity other than a single REG bundle.

  • FFS, whether channel estimation can be reused and how to count overlapping CCEs of different candidates in Alt.2a and Alt.3.

  • FFS, how to support interleaved and/or non-interleaved CORESET in Alt.2a and Alt.3.

  • Note: If there is no consensus to support an alternative, Alt 1 would be supported.
