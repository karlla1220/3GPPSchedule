MSG4-triggered CSI/TRS/SRS must be in the initial BWP. Rel-20 early CSI/SRS/CSI-RS is not supported for the case that BWP switching is configured in RRCSetup/RRCResume message contained in MSG4 PDSCH.

MSG4-triggered CSI/TRS/SRS is transmitted/received in the DL/UL BWP after UE completes RRC configuration based on RRC setup/resume message, i.e.,

  • If BWP switching is triggered by a RRC setup/resume message, the MSG4-triggered CSI/TRS/SRS is in the first active DL/UL BWP configured in the RRC setup/resume message. Otherwise, the MSG4-triggered CSI/TRS/SRS is in the initial DL/UL BWP.
