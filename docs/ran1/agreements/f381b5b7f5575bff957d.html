Agreement

For study of multiple carriers where for initial access a UE can utilize resources for UL (Msg1, etc) or for both UL (Msg1, etc.) and DL (Msg2, etc.) in one carrier other than the carrier where the UE obtains the system information providing configuration for those resources, consider the following potential scenarios for the set of candidate physical carriers for which resources are provided for initial access:

  • S1: 1 DL physical carrier + N UL physical carriers, where N > 1

  • S2: M DL physical carriers + 1 UL physical carrier, where M > 1

  • S3: M DL physical carriers + N UL physical carriers, where M > 1 and N > 1

Note 1: The carrier where the UE obtains the system information and other candidate physical carrier(s) are counted above.

Note 2: For a given UE and a given initial access procedure, UE may use only a subset of the candidate physical carrier.

Note 3: The inclusion of a scenario for study does not imply agreement to support it. The need and applicable use cases/deployment scenarios for each scenario are subject to further study.

Note 4: For S2 and S3, M DL physical carriers may include carrier(s) only providing reference signals for initial-access UL transmissions on candidate UL carriers.

Agreement

For S1: 1 DL physical carrier + N UL physical carriers, where N > 1,

  • For UC-1 (UL coverage enhancement / DL–UL coverage imbalance), compared with single-carrier initial access on a mid-/high-frequency carrier, idle mode multi-carrier operation which enables Msg1/Msg3 transmission on a lower-frequency UL carrier while retaining DL reception on the mid-/high-frequency carrier can provide additional benefit in terms of UL initial access coverage for UEs with sufficient DL coverage but insufficient UL coverage on the mid-/high-frequency carrier.

    • This is applicable at least to the following scenario(s) for the set of candidate physical carriers for which resources are provided for initial access

      • S1: 1 DL physical carrier + N UL physical carriers, where N > 1

      • FFS applicability to S3: M DL physical carriers + N UL physical carriers, where M > 1 and N > 1

        • Only one of the M DL physical carriers is monitored from UE perspective for system information, paging, DL (Msg2, etc.) reception

        • The other DL physical carriers provide reference signals for initial-access UL transmissions on candidate UL carriers only

Agreement

For UC-1 (UL coverage enhancement / DL–UL coverage imbalance), compared with single-carrier initial access on a mid-/high-frequency carrier, idle mode multi-carrier operation which enables Msg1/Msg3 transmission on a lower-frequency UL carrier while retaining DL reception on the mid-/high-frequency carrier can provide additional benefit in terms of UL initial access coverage for UEs with sufficient DL coverage but insufficient UL coverage on the mid-/high-frequency carrier.

  • This is applicable at least to the following scenario(s) for the set of candidate physical carriers for which resources are provided for initial access

    • S1: 1 DL physical carrier + N UL physical carriers, where N > 1

    • FFS applicability to S3: M DL physical carriers + N UL physical carriers, where M > 1 and N > 1
