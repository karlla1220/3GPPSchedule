FL proposal [P1-4-1r2] group-specific DCI

6GR supports the following DCIs

  • UE-specific DCI,

  • Cell-specific DCI and DCI for random access procedure, and

Further study whether group-specific DCI is needed by considering potential use cases, e.g.,

  • UL TPC

  • Multi-UE scheduling

  • Energy-saving related indications

  • SRS triggering

  • CORESET duration indication

  • Other potential use case is not precluded

Note: support of some use cases above will not be discussed/decided in AI10.5.4.1

Note: group-specific DCI corresponds to group-common information or group of UEs’ information indication.

On whether/how to support the minimum DCI-to-PDSCH scheduling offset > 0 slot for single-stage DCI, study at least the following options for the triggering of dynamic adaptation of the minimum DCI-to-PDSCH scheduling offset:

  • FFS: handling of USS and CSS
