Regarding temporal domain DL Tx beam prediction and management on multi-TRP/cell(intra/inter-cell) are summarized as follows.

  • Note: The observations may be further updated based on additional inputs from companies in future meetings.

For temporal domain DL Tx beam prediction on multi-TRP/cell(intra/inter-cell), the following two Cases are considered:

- Case-1: the cells/TRPs corresponding to the predicted beam(s) (Set-A) are within the set of cells/TRPs corresponding to the measured beam(s) (Set-B).

- Case-2: at least one of the cells/TRPs corresponding to the predicted beam(s) (Set-A) are outside the set of cells/TRPs corresponding to the measured beam(s) (Set-B).

Note that in the following evaluations, ideal measurements are assumed:

- Beams could be measured regardless of their SNR.

- No measurement error.

- Measured in a single-time instance (within a channel-coherence time interval).

- No quantization for the L1-RSRP measurements.

- No constraint on UCI payload overhead for full report of the L1-RSRP measurements of Set B for NW-side models are assumed.

For temporal domain DL Tx beam prediction on multi-TRP/cell(intra/inter-cell), for Case 1, when Set B = Set A,

- Average beam prediction accuracy over all predicted time instances

- Top-1 DL Tx beam prediction accuracy:

  • Evaluation results from ZTE indicate that AI/ML solution can achieve about 85% Top-1 beam prediction accuracy, for 40ms to 160ms prediction time.

  • Evaluation results from NTT DOCOMO indicate that AI/ML solution can achieve about 84%~95% Top-1 beam prediction accuracy, for 20ms to 160ms prediction time.

  • Evaluation results from Nokia indicate about 71%–75% for 40 ms to 240 ms prediction time (with 40 ms measurement periodicity), and about 64%–70% for 80 ms to 480 ms prediction time (with 80 ms periodicity).

  • Evaluation results from Huawei indicate that AI/ML solution can achieve about 23.99% Top-1 beam prediction accuracy, for 160ms to 1440ms prediction time. With the confidence level reported by the UE, the Top-1 beam prediction accuracy increases to 34.42%.

- Top-2 DL Tx beam prediction accuracy:

  • Evaluation results from ZTE indicate that AI/ML solution can achieve about 95% Top-2 beam prediction accuracy, for 40ms to 160ms prediction time.

  • Evaluation results from Nokia indicate about 88%–91% for 40 ms to 240 ms prediction time (with 40 ms measurement periodicity), and about 82%–87% for 80 ms to 480 ms prediction time (with 80 ms periodicity).

- Top-3 DL Tx beam prediction accuracy:

  • Evaluation results from Huawei indicate that AI/ML solution can achieve about 77.24% Top-3 beam prediction accuracy, for 160ms to 1440ms prediction time. With the confidence level reported by the UE, the Top-1 beam prediction accuracy increases to 83.52%.

- Top-4 DL Tx beam prediction accuracy:

  • Evaluation results from ZTE indicate that AI/ML solution can achieve about 99% Top-4 beam prediction accuracy, for 40ms to 160ms prediction time.

  • Evaluation results from NTT DOCOMO indicate that AI/ML solution can achieve about 98%~99% Top-4 beam prediction accuracy, for 20ms to 160ms prediction time.

  • Evaluation results from Nokia indicate about 96%–97% for 40 ms to 240 ms prediction time (with 40 ms measurement periodicity), and about 92%–95% for 80 ms to 480 ms prediction time (with 80 ms periodicity).

- Average L1-RSRP difference between Top-1 predicted beam and genie-aided beam

  • Evaluation results from [Huawei, NTT DOCOMO, and ZTE] indicate that the L1-RSRP difference between the Top-1 predicted beam and the genie-aided beam of the AI/ML solution is about 0.05~0.71 dB.

  • Evaluation results from Nokia indicate that the mean L1-RSRP difference between the Top-1 predicted beam and the genie-aided beam of the AI/ML solution is about 0.97–1.98 dB (for 40 ms to 480 ms prediction time).

Regarding DL-RS multiplexing for intra-symbol beam sweeping are summarized as follows.

  • Note: The observations may be further updated based on additional inputs from companies in future meetings.

For evaluation purpose, the following candidates on DL-RS multiplexing regarding intra-symbol beam sweeping are categorized:

  • Option-1 (FDMed): Frequency-domain/FDMed DL RS(s) for NW Tx beam sweeping, i.e., multiplexing the frequency resource(s) corresponding to Rf >1 DL RS(s):

    • [xiaomi, BeammWave] report evaluation results on the following sub-option:

      • Option-1a: The respective frequency resource(s) corresponding to Rf >1 DL RS(s) are split into different RBs/subbands.

    • [Qualcomm, ZTE, xiaomi, BeammWave, Kyocera] report evaluation results on the following sub-option:

      • Option-1b: The respective frequency resource(s) corresponding to Rf >1 DL RS(s) are split into different REs but in a same RB/RB group.

  • Option-2 (CDMed): Sequence-domain/CDMed DL RS(s) for NW Tx beam sweeping, i.e., multiplexing the sequence(s) corresponding to Rc >1 DL RS(s):

    • [Qualcomm, ZTE, xiaomi, BeammWave, Kyocera] report evaluation results on the following sub-option:

      • Option-2a: Each of DL RS can correspond to the respective OCC (e.g., FD/TD-OCC) in a localized or a spaced RE pair/group

        • Option-2a-1 (port-level granularity): Rc DL RS(s) correspond to different DL RS ports in a DL RS resource;

        • Option-2a-2 (resource level granularity): Rc DL RS(s) correspond to different DL RS resource(s);

    • [Qualcomm, BeammWave] report evaluation results on the following sub-option:

      • Option-2b: Each of Rc DL RS(s) can be scrambled by different scrambling sequence roots (NR inter-cell SSS like).

  • Option-3 (TDMed): Sub-symbol/TDMed for UE Rx beam sweeping, i.e., sub-symbol(s) (e.g., time-domain multiplexing within a symbol) measured by a respective Rx beam can be achieved by the following candidate(s) for DL RS:

    • [Apple] report revaluation results on the following sub-option:

      • Option-3a: DL RS(s) corresponds to different DL RS ports in a DL RS resource, where each of DL RS ports can correspond to FD-OCC with a spacing of X REs within a RB.

    • [Apple, BeammWave, IDC] report evaluation results on the following sub-option:

      • Option-3b: Rt ≥1 DL RS(s) corresponds to respective DL RS ports in a DL RS resource, where each of DL RS ports can correspond to respective REs with a spacing of X REs within a RB.

    • [IDC] report evaluation results on the following sub-option:

      • Option-3c: DL RS(s) are generated using additional DFT processing (analogous to DFT-S-OFDM) to support sub-symbol UE Rx beam sweeping.

    • [Qualcomm] report evaluation results on the following sub-option:

      • Option-3d: DL RS(s) corresponds to different DL RS ports in a DL RS resource, where each of DL RS ports can correspond to FD-OCC with adjacent REs.

    • Note: There is UE assumption of no DL signal transmitting in the same symbol with a certain bandwidth

    • Note: The OFDM symbol and its SCS are unchanged in such case.

For each candidate of DL-RS multiplexing regarding intra-symbol beam sweeping, regarding beam-detection accuracy and beam-sweeping overhead/latency:

  • 5 companies’ simulation results demonstrated that Option-1 (FDMed) and Option-2 (CDMed) approaches are comparable to inter-symbol approach over the applicable SNR range regarding beam-detection accuracy, while significantly reducing the number of OFDM symbols for NW Tx beam sweeping by a factor of Rf and Rc, respectively, from 2 to 12. [Qualcomm, BeammWave, xiaomi, ZTE, Kyocera]

    • Evaluation results from [Qualcomm] indicate that, at Rf =Rc=4 and 0dB SINR, both Option-1b (FDMed) and Option-2b (CDMed) approach achieve 88% beam detection accuracy, when the inter-symbol approach, as a benchmark, achieves 92% beam detection accuracy.

    • Evaluation results from [BeammWave] indicate that, at Rf =Rc=8 and SNR from -10dB to 10dB, Option-1a (FDMed), Option-1b (FDMed), Option-2a (CDMed) and Option-2b(CDMed) approaches achieve 66.8%~89.3%, 68.2%~97.2%, 68.1%~96.3% and 67.1%~94.1% beam detection accuracy (top-1, i.e. exactly the best Tx-Rx beam pair, without margin), respectively, when the inter-symbol approach with the same RS energy per beam, as a benchmark, achieves 67.4%~97.4% beam detection accuracy. With 1dB L1-RSRP margin, the corresponding values are 80.8%~98.5%, 82.6%~100%, 83.3%~100% and 82.2%~100%, respectively, when the inter-symbol approach with the same RS energy per beam achieves 82.5%~100%.

    • Evaluation results from [xiaomi] indicate that, at Rf =2~4, Rc=2~4, and SNR from -10dB to 0dB, Option-1a (FDMed), Option-1b (FDMed) and Option-2a (CDMed) approaches achieve 94.3%~100%, ~100% and ~100% beam detection accuracy with 1dB L1-RSRP margin, respectively, when the inter-symbol approach, as a benchmark, achieves ~100% beam detection accuracy with 1dB L1-RSRP margin. Without L1-RSRP margin, Option-1a (FDMed), Option-1b (FDMed) and Option-2a (CDMed) approaches achieve 78.3%~91.6%, 93%~96.7% and 93.4%~97.3% beam detection accuracy, respectively, when the inter-symbol approach, as a benchmark, achieves 93.2~96.8% beam detection accuracy.

    • Evaluation results from [ZTE] indicate that, at Rf =Rc=4 and SNR from -10dB to 10dB, Option-1b (FDMed) and Option-2a (CDMed) approaches achieve 92.4%~98.4%, and 95.4%~99.0% beam detection accuracy, respectively, when the inter-symbol approach, as a benchmark, achieves 91.8%~98.6% beam detection accuracy.

    • Evaluation results from [Kyocera] indicate that, at SNR from -10dB to 10dB, Option-1b (FDMed, Rf =8), Option-1b (FDMed) + Option-2a (CDMed) (i.e., Rf =4 & Rc=2, Rf =2 & Rc=4) approaches achieve 73.6%~98.5%，73.7%~98.4% beam detection accuracy, respectively, when the inter-symbol approach, as a benchmark, achieves 73.8%~99.3% beam detection accuracy.

  • Besides, among those sub-options among Option-1(FDMed) and Option-2(CDMed),

    • Evaluation results from [xiaomi, ZTE] indicates that Option-2a/CDMed approach achieves better beam-detection accuracy than Option-1b/FDMed, especially for low SINR.

      • Evaluation results from [xiaomi] indicate that, when Rf = Rc is from 2 to 4 and SNR is -20dB, Option-1b (FDMed) approach can achieve ~79.0% beam detection accuracy with 1dB L1-RSRP margin, and then Option-2a(CDMed) approach can achieve ~85%. Without L1-RSRP margin, Option-1b (FDMed) approach can achieve ~67.3% beam detection accuracy, and then Option-2a(CDMed) approach can achieve ~71.3%.

      • Evaluation results from [ZTE] indicate that, when Rf = Rc = 4 and SNR is from -10dB to 0dB, Option-1b (FDMed) approach can achieve 92.4%~98.4% beam detection accuracy, and then Option-2a(CDMed) approach can achieve 95.4%~99.0%.

    • Evaluation results from [Qualcomm, BeammWave] indicate that Option-1b (FDMed) and Option-2a (CDMed) approaches achieve comparable beam-detection accuracy at the same RS energy per beam.

    • Evaluation results from [xiaomi, BeammWave] indicates that, regarding sub-options in Option-1 (FDMed), Option-1b (FDMed) approach is more robust to frequency-selective fading than Option-1a (FDMed) approach.

      • Evaluation results from [xiaomi] indicates that, when Rf = Rc increases from 2 to 4, beam detection accuracy with 1dB L1-RSRP margin of Option-1a (FDMed) approach reduces from 99.3%~100% to 94.3%~95.6%, where SINR is from -10dB to 0dB; but, in those cases, Option-1b (FDMed) can keep ~100% beam detection accuracy with 1dB L1-RSRP margin.

      • Evaluation results from [BeammWave] indicates that, when Delay spread increases from 30ns to 100ns, at 10dB SNR, beam detection accuracy of Option-1a (FDMed) approach reduces from 95.4% to 89.3%, while Option-1b (FDMed) only reduces from 97.5% to 97.2%.

  • Then, for Option-3 (TDMed), assuming 4~6 sub-symbol(s) in time domain for UE Rx beam sweeping, evaluation results from [Apple, BeammWave, IDC] indicates that Option-3 (TDMed) approach can achieve comparable beam-detection accuracy compared with the inter-symbol approach, while reducing the number of OFDM symbols for UE Rx beam sweeping by a factor of the number of UE Rx beams per OFDM symbol, and evaluation results from [Qualcomm] indicates that Option-3 (TDMed) may introduce severe beam-detection accuracy degradation, especially for low SNR.

    • Evaluation results from [Apple] indicates that, at the same number of DL RS OFDM symbols, Option-3 (TDMed) approach with 10 ms DL RS periodicity is within about 0.2–0.6 dB SNR loss compared with the ideal Rx beam, while inter-symbol UE Rx beam sweeping with 40 ms periodicity has about 1–2 dB SNR loss.

    • Evaluation results from [Apple] indicates that, for a 2-port DL RS with pilots on every 6th subcarrier, Option-3a and Option-3b give 2.1% beam-alignment mismatch and throughput close to the ideal Rx beam, while FD-CDM on adjacent subcarriers (NR CDM-2 layout) gives 68.7% mismatch and is about 2–2.5 dB worse in throughput.

    • Evaluation results from [Qualcomm] indicates that Option-3d (TDMed) approach requires about 4 dB higher SNR than the legacy UE Rx beam sweeping for the same Tx–Rx pair beam-detection error rate, which results in a throughput degradation of 62 % at −5 dB.

    • Evaluation results from [IDC] indicates that, regarding sub-options in Option-3 (TDMed), Option-3c can provide measurement performance comparable to Option-3a/3b, with additional flexibility in frequency-domain resource allocation and time-domain waveform generation.
